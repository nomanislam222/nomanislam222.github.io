About

Researcher, engineer, and teacher — in that order of curiosity.

I am a Computer Science graduate from American International University-Bangladesh, majoring in Software Engineering, where I finished with a CGPA of 3.93, Magna Cum Laude, and the Dean's Award in every semester I studied.

My work sits at the meeting point of three questions: how do people actually use software, how can large language models be directed reliably, and how much of computing's cost — financial and environmental — is avoidable. Those questions produced five research works: UX maturity across Bangladeshi digital firms, a comparative study of rent-a-house platforms, prompt engineering and human interaction in LLMs, cost optimisation in microservice architecture, and a bridge framework for closing the UX design gap in small companies.

Alongside research I lead IT at The New School Dhaka — building the Computer Science syllabus against the Cambridge curriculum, running the school's networking and systems infrastructure, and teaching ICT and AI to students who will inherit all of it. Before that I shipped a production Laravel management system for a food-manufacturing brand, which is where I learned that elegant architecture means nothing if the person on the factory floor cannot use it.

03

Research

Five works across HCI, large language models, software engineering and cloud economics.

JournalHCI · UX Analysis

Comparative Analysis of Rent-a-House Platforms: Identifying Disparities and Recommending Enhancements for the Bangladeshi Market

A comparative evaluation of housing-rental platforms serving Bangladesh, surfacing the interaction and information gaps between them and setting out concrete design enhancements for the local market.

Supervisor Dr. Mohammad Mahmudul Hasan · Associate Professor, AIUB
ConferenceUX Maturity · HCI · SE

UX Maturity in Bangladeshi Digital Firms: A Multi-Case Assessment and Roadmap

A multi-case assessment of how UX practice actually matures inside Bangladeshi digital firms, ending in a staged roadmap organisations can use to move up the maturity ladder deliberately rather than by accident.

Supervisor Dr. Mohammad Mahmudul Hasan · Associate Professor, AIUB
JournalLLMs · Prompt Engineering

Prompt Engineering & Human Interaction in LLMs: Challenges & Opportunities

Examines the linguistic limitations of large language models, categorises prompt-engineering techniques, evaluates NLP model behaviour, and maps the modes through which humans and LLMs actually interact — with particular attention to Bangla.

Supervisor Prof. Dr. Firoz Mridha · Director, AMIRL
ThesisCloud · Microservices · Cost

Reducing Infrastructure & Operational Costs in Microservice Architecture: Optimizing Resource Efficiency through Cost-Effective Strategies

Undergraduate thesis on where microservice deployments waste resources and money, and which cost-effective strategies recover that efficiency without giving up the architecture's benefits.

Supervisor Taslimur Rahman · Lecturer, Dept. of CSE, AIUB
JournalUX Framework · SE

Bridge Framework for UX Design Gap: An Approach for Small Companies to Produce High-Quality Products

Proposes a framework that lets small companies — without dedicated UX teams or budgets — close the gap between what they build and what users need, and still ship high-quality products.

Supervisor Taslimur Rahman · Lecturer, Dept. of CSE, AIUB

Current research direction: green computing and carbon-footprint reduction across the Software Development Life Cycle, as a researcher with the Department of Computer Science, AIUB.
