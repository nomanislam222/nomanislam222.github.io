01 — About

Researcher, engineer, and the person who keeps the systems running.

I graduated in Computer Science from American International University-Bangladesh, majoring in Software Engineering, finishing with a CGPA of 3.93, Magna Cum Laude, and the Dean's Award in every semester I studied.

My work sits where three questions meet: how do people actually use software, how can large language models be directed reliably, and how much of computing's cost — financial and environmental — is avoidable. Those questions produced five research works across HCI, LLMs, software engineering and cloud economics.

Alongside research I run two things that need someone to be accountable for them: the IT function of a school, and a consultancy I founded. Both taught me the same lesson the Laravel system I shipped for a food manufacturer taught me — elegant architecture means nothing if the person on the floor can't use it.

04 — Research

Five works across HCI, LLMs and cloud economics.

JournalHCI · UX Analysis

Comparative Analysis of Rent-a-House Platforms: Identifying Disparities and Recommending Enhancements for the Bangladeshi Market

A comparative evaluation of housing-rental platforms serving Bangladesh, surfacing the interaction and information gaps between them and setting out concrete design enhancements for the local market.

Supervisor Dr. Mohammad Mahmudul Hasan · Associate Professor, AIUB
ConferenceUX Maturity · HCI · SE

UX Maturity in Bangladeshi Digital Firms: A Multi-Case Assessment and Roadmap

A multi-case assessment of how UX practice actually matures inside Bangladeshi digital firms, ending in a staged roadmap organisations can use to climb the maturity ladder deliberately rather than by accident.

Supervisor Dr. Mohammad Mahmudul Hasan · Associate Professor, AIUB
JournalLLMs · Prompt Engineering

Prompt Engineering & Human Interaction in LLMs: Challenges & Opportunities

Examines the linguistic limitations of large language models, categorises prompt-engineering techniques, evaluates NLP model behaviour, and maps the modes through which humans and LLMs actually interact — with particular attention to Bangla.

Supervisor Prof. Dr. Firoz Mridha · Director, AMIRL
ThesisCloud · Microservices · Cost

Reducing Infrastructure & Operational Costs in Microservice Architecture: Optimizing Resource Efficiency through Cost-Effective Strategies

Undergraduate thesis on where microservice deployments waste resources and money, and which cost-effective strategies recover that efficiency without giving up the architecture's benefits.

Supervisor Taslimur Rahman · Lecturer, Dept. of CSE, AIUB
JournalUX Framework · SE

Bridge Framework for UX Design Gap: An Approach for Small Companies to Produce High-Quality Products

Proposes a framework that lets small companies — without dedicated UX teams or budgets — close the gap between what they build and what users need, and still ship high-quality products.

Supervisor Taslimur Rahman · Lecturer, Dept. of CSE, AIUB

Current direction: green computing and carbon-footprint reduction across the Software Development Life Cycle, as a researcher with the Department of Computer Science, AIUB.
